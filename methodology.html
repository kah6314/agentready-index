Evidence

The crawl (2026-10-13) used curl with a bot user agent and recorded: home status and size, robots.txt status, the count of full disallows for GPTBot/ClaudeBot, and mentions of AI agents. The raw evidence.csv and robots files are published under data/raw/. The crawl did not record captcha/denied keywords; blocks are inferred from status codes (403, 429, 202 empty body) instead.

Known data issues

  • United: request timed out. The saved robots file duplicated Southwest's and was excluded.
  • Hyatt, Resy: saved robots files were HTML, not robots.txt, and were excluded.
  • Amazon: HTTP 202 with an empty body is treated as a bot challenge.
  • Single run from one network; results may differ by IP and time.

Limits

Dimensions 2-7 rely on public information, not live testing, and may be out of date. Scores are not endorsements or audits. Corrections welcome; see the change log.
