FAQ

Which online stores are most ready for AI agent shoppers?

As of Oct 14, 2026, the top three in the AgentReady Index are Allbirds (86/100), Instacart (76/100), Walmart (73/100).

Which stores are least ready for AI agents?

The lowest scores are Hyatt (43/100), United Airlines (39/100), Airbnb (37/100), mostly because they block AI crawlers or return bot challenges.

What is the AgentReady Index?

A free, open 0-100 score of how easily an AI agent can browse, buy, book or get service from 21 major online stores, built by Kevin Harmon and updated weekly.

How is the AgentReady score calculated?

Seven dimensions: bot access (20), agent checkout (20), structured paths such as APIs, MCP and agent payments (20), and email, text, chat and book-online channels (10 each). See the methodology.

Can I use the data?

Yes. All scores are open under CC BY 4.0 at /data/sites.json. Please cite "AgentReady Index".
