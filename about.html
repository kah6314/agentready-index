About

The AgentReady Index tracks how easily AI agents can shop, book and get service from major online stores.

Why

More buying will start with an assistant, not a search box. My bet is that businesses that make it easy for AI agents to find, compare and buy from them will win share from those that block agents. This index is a way to measure that, in the open, week by week.

Who

Built by Kevin Harmon, a business-school professor who studies technology and markets. Data collection and site builds are automated with AI agents; scoring rules and judgment calls are mine.

How

Each store gets a 0-100 score across seven dimensions: bot access, agent checkout, structured paths (APIs, MCP, agent payments), and four customer channels. See the methodology. Everything is published as open data: /data/sites.json (CC BY 4.0).

Status and corrections

Scores are preliminary and have not yet been verified by live checkout tests. If you work at one of these companies and think a score is wrong, send a public source and the score will be re-checked and logged in the change log.
